## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

 Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

  Farm2Home is a digital marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers, eliminating the need for multiple intermediaries. Farmers can easily register and upload their agricultural products, including product names, quantities, prices, and availability. Customers can browse available products, compare prices, place orders, and make secure online payments through UPI. The platform also provides logistics support by coordinating product collection from nearby village hubs and arranging deliveries to customers. Additionally, AI-based demand forecasting helps farmers understand market demand, while intelligent route optimization improves transportation efficiency. Farm2Home aims to provide better prices for farmers, affordable agricultural products for consumers, and a more efficient agricultural supply chain.

 How it addresses the problem


Farmers sell their agricultural products directly to consumers and bulk buyers, reducing dependence on intermediaries and avoiding unnecessary commission charges.

The platform displays current mandi prices and uses AI to suggest fair selling prices, helping farmers receive better value for their products.

The AI-based demand forecasting system predicts future demand for agricultural products, helping farmers plan their sales, reduce unsold stock, and minimize food wastage.

Village collection hubs collect agricultural products from multiple nearby farmers, making bulk transportation more affordable and efficient.

Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers at lower prices because the supply chain involves fewer intermediaries.

The voice-based product listing feature allows farmers to upload their products using voice commands in their own language, making the platform easier to use for farmers who are not comfortable typing or using complex applications.

 Innovation and uniqueness of the solution



Farm2home provides voice-first access in local languages, allowing farmers to use the platform easily without requiring advanced digital knowledge or typing skills.

The platform introduces village collection hubs that collect agricultural products from multiple small and marginal farmers, making transportation more affordable and efficient.

It combines direct farmer-to-consumer selling, AI-based price recommendations, demand forecasting, and intelligent route optimization within a single digital marketplace, creating an efficient and farmer-friendly agricultural supply chain.

## Technical Approach

1. **Technologies to Be Used:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Flask, SQLite, AI and Machine Learning libraries, Speech-to-Text technology, UPI payment integration, and Google Maps API.

2. **Methodology and Process for Implementation:** Develop a web-based digital marketplace with separate farmer and customer interfaces. Implement voice-based product listing in local languages using speech recognition. Store product and user information in a database. Enable customers to browse products, place orders, and make UPI payments. Integrate AI-based price recommendations, demand forecasting, and route optimization. Use village collection hubs to coordinate product collection and delivery. Develop a working prototype to demonstrate the complete process.

## Feasibility and Viability

Farm2Home is feasible using existing web technologies, AI tools, and digital payment systems. It can be developed as a low-cost platform and expanded gradually to support more farmers and customers. Challenges such as internet access, digital literacy, and transportation can be addressed through voice-based access, local collection hubs, and logistics partnerships.

## Impact and Benefits

Farm2Home helps farmers earn better profits by connecting them directly with consumers and bulk buyers. It provides customers with fresh agricultural products at affordable prices while reducing unnecessary intermediaries. The platform improves transportation efficiency, reduces food wastage through demand forecasting, supports rural farmers through digital technology, and creates a more transparent and sustainable agricultural supply chain.
